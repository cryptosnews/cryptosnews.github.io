About Crypto Evidence Daily

Crypto Evidence Daily is an independent newsroom covering crypto markets, protocol changes and public policy. We explain what happened, how it works and whose economics change, with reporting built for researchers and analysts.

We prioritize primary documents, reproducible on-chain evidence and attributable sources. We distinguish reported facts from estimates and analysis, disclose relevant conflicts, and keep commercial relationships separate from editorial decisions. We correct material errors promptly and explain substantive changes in the affected article.
